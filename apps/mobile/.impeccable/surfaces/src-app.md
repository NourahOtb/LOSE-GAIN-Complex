# Surface brief: LOSE | GAIN app (parent + coach)

Scope: parent home, registration + price, kid profile, coach view. Mode: operate. Demo Wednesday 2026-11-18.
Audience/job: parents see what's next, sessions left, renewals; coaches see kids, health, levels/belts and add stripes.
Constraints: Arabic RTL + English, light/dark, large tap targets, black/white brand, one accent per sport.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each sport is shown in its own native notation: swimming as pool lanes with lane-line T-ends and a pace-clock time, jiu-jitsu as a belt with taped stripes. Refuses the generic card-with-progress-bar booking app.

OWN-WORLD: White tile ground (#FFFFFF, pool tint #E8F4F7), black lane-line ink #111, grey #666 labels. Swimming accent #1E7FA8, jiu-jitsu accent #D2552B (used only in sport marks, lanes, calendar). Belt colours are the real belt colours (white, grey, yellow, orange, green) with a black bar and white tape stripes. Logo lockup: thin LOSE | bold GAIN, COMPLEX in very wide spacing. Type: Barlow / Barlow Condensed for numerals and caps labels (Arabic: Readex Pro).

STORY: Parent opens app → sees next class on the pace clock → this week as lanes, one per kid → sessions left as big condensed numerals → renews or registers in a few steps with price visible. Coach opens a kid → sees health first → belt with stripes and dates → taps "Add stripe", picks date.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: logo lockup left, greeting right. Pace-clock panel (pool tint) with next class. Week grid Sat–Thu, one lane per kid, class marks on the lane. Sessions-left numerals per kid. Renewal alert bar in black. Bottom tab bar: Home, Kids, Register, Inbox.
